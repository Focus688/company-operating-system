# 05 · Resource Foundation: People, Finance, Materials, Data

> The foundation answers: **what sustains the business?**
> People come first, then the work; data comes first, then decisions.
> ⚠️ "Data" is the fourth resource of the modern enterprise — many companies miss it.

---

## 1. People (human resources)

### 1.1 Select, develop, deploy, retain

| Stage | Core actions | Key point |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Select | Hiring / screening | Above the iceberg: capability; below the iceberg: values (see Chapter 10) |
| Develop | Training / mentoring | Capability can be trained; values almost never change |
| Deploy | Role matching | Play to people's strengths, tolerate their weaknesses |
| Retain | Pay + growth + meaning | All three layers — money / promotion / mission — are indispensable |

### 1.2 ⚠️ Leak plugging

- **Talent pipeline / succession planning**: no critical role should depend on a single person (A/B roles)
- **Incentives**: a dual track of bonus / promotion + equity (to bind core talent)
- **Performance management**: goals → review → feedback → improvement (closed loop)

---

## 2. Finance (accounting / tax)

### 2.1 The three financial statements

| Statement | Answers | Key point |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Income statement | Are we making money? | Revenue − cost = profit |
| Balance sheet | How much do we own? | Assets − liabilities = net assets |
| Cash flow statement | Are we alive? | **Cash is the line between life and death** |

### 2.2 ⚠️ Cash flow is the lifeline of manufacturing

**Profit is an opinion; cash is a fact.**

Three payment-term cycles decide life or death:
```
AR payment terms (how long customers owe you)   → shorter is better
Inventory days (how long goods sit)             → faster is better
AP payment terms (how long you owe suppliers)   → longer is better (interest-free financing)
```

- Case: many companies die with "a good-looking income statement but a broken cash flow"
- Management actions: aging schedules, collection procedures, inventory red lines, cash flow forecasting (next 3-6 months)

### 2.3 Tax planning (especially cross-border)

- Export tax rebates (manufacturing)
- Transfer pricing (related-party transactions at market rates)
- Hong Kong/Singapore structures (keeping profit in low-tax jurisdictions)
- ⚠️ This is not tax evasion — it is compliant optimization

## 3. Materials (procurement / supply chain)

### 3.1 The full supplier lifecycle

```
Sourcing → Qualification → Quoting & negotiation → Ordering → Performance review → Phase-out
```

| Stage | Key point |
|:---|:---|
| Sourcing | At least 2-3 candidates |
| Qualification | Samples + audit + small-batch trial |
| Negotiation | Annual price-downs, payment terms, volume discounts |
| Performance | Score on quality / delivery / price / cooperativeness |
| Phase-out | Swap out after repeated misses |

### 3.2 ⚠️ Supply chain resilience

- **Dual-source** critical materials (never bet on a single supplier)
- Lock in long-lead-time materials early
- Supply-interruption plan (alternate materials / alternate suppliers / safety stock)
- Monitor geopolitical / price-increase / logistics risks

## 4. Data (IT systems / data foundation) ⚠️ essential for modern enterprises

### 4.1 The system stack

| System | Solves | Manufacturing example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ERP | Integrated business-finance (orders-procurement-production-inventory-finance) | Kingdee / Yonyou / Odoo / ERPNext |
| MES | Manufacturing execution (work orders-operations-yield) | Shop floor |
| CRM | Customer management | Sales |
| WMS | Warehouse management | Inventory |
| BI | Data analytics / dashboard | Decisions |

### 4.2 The business dashboard (management accounting)

**True profit by product / customer / project** — the basis for pricing and for cutting orders.

```
Check daily / weekly:
├─ Revenue (by product / customer)
├─ Gross profit (by product / customer)
├─ Cash flow
├─ Inventory (value + turnover)
└─ Open orders (delivery risk)
```

- Case: many companies "feel profitable" but don't know which product makes money — management accounting on ERP data makes it instantly clear
- ⚠️ More data is not better — build **what decisions actually need to see**

---

## 5. Cost structure (people / finance / materials mix)

| Industry | Typical cost structure |
|:---|:---|
| Manufacturing | Materials 50-70% + labor 10-20% + overhead 20-30% |
| Services | Labor 60%+ |
| Trading | Goods + freight + cost of capital |
| Software | Labor 60-80% + cloud |

- Management action: activity-based costing to get true costs → pricing / order-cutting backed by data

---

> 📌 **One-line summary**: Four foundation resources — people (select/develop/deploy/retain), finance (three statements + cash flow), materials (supply chain), and data (systems + dashboard). Cash flow and true gross margin are the two lifelines of a manufacturing company.
